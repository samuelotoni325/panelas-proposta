The call sheet of a life in kitchens.

A film set runs on a call sheet: who, where, what happens next. Marcia spent 15 years in that world. Everything else she learned at home.

  1. Scene 1 · Int. Family kitchen, Brazil

    Mother, grandmother, stove.

    Born and raised in Brazil, Marcia hones her skills alongside her mother and grandmother. The best moments of her life happen in the kitchen.

  2. Scene 2 · Ext. America

    Over two decades ago.

    She moves to America, bringing not only Brazilian flavors but Italian, Mediterranean, Asian and South American cooking too.

  3. Scene 3 · Int. Film sets, Greater LA

    15 years in the movie industry.

    Catering, the movie industry and private events across Greater LA.

  4. Scene 4 · Int. Panelas, Redondo Beach

    Since 2017, the South Bay.

    For Marcia, the kitchen is a space where love, authenticity and empathy come to life. Her work, she believes, is about serving people.

Everything at Panelas is designed to make you feel right at home.

An atmosphere of warmth

Children’s laughter, young couples, the union of friends. Whether you’re a longtime fan of Brazilian cuisine or simply missing the comfort of a home-cooked meal, Panelas is the place.

Our promise

We believe that dining is more than just eating. It’s about creating memories, sharing love, and connecting with people.
